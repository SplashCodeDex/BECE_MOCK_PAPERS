Ghana’s best BECE Mocks for schools and homes
by
Wisdom K.E Hammond
·
September 25, 2026
Education-News Consult has positioned its BECE Mock examination services as a major preparation option for schools and parents, offering both school-based and home-based mock examinations through its Skulmanager.com platform.
The examination consultancy says its services are designed to help Junior High School students prepare for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) through mock questions based on its projected topics for each BECE year.
The services are available in two main forms: the
BECE School Mock Service
for schools and the
BECE Home Mock Service
for parents who want their children assessed from home.
Education-News Consult offers two BECE Mock services
According to Education-News Consult, its BECE Mock services have been structured to meet the needs of both schools and individual parents.
The School Mock Service provides schools with examination materials that can be used to conduct mock examinations for their students. Schools can receive the papers as soft copies or opt for printed hard copies.
The organisation says schools that choose its marking service can also have students’ scripts marked and receive reports on their performance. Other schools may receive the examination materials and conduct the mock examination independently.
Education-News Consult says both its school and home mock services are built around its
yearly projected BECE topics
.
Home BECE Mock brings examination practice to parents and students
A key feature of the services is the Home Mock system operated through the Skulmanager.com platform.
Education-News Consult says the service allows parents to turn their homes into examination centres where students can sit the mock under examination conditions.
After the student completes the examination, parents can upload the scripts through the school portal for marking.
The organisation says parents can then check their children’s results online, download the marked scripts and obtain a report on the student’s performance.
This gives parents a way to assess their children’s preparation without necessarily taking them to a physical mock examination centre.
The Home Mock Service page for more information is
https://skulmanager.com/Homemock/index.php
Education-News Consult highlights its projected BECE topics
Education-News Consult says one of the distinguishing features of its mock services is its focus on projected BECE questions and topics.
The organisation describes itself as an examination consultancy service that combines its mock examination work with projections of questions and topics expected to be accessed by the West African Examinations Council (WAEC) at the BECE.
Education-News Consult puts its
prediction accuracy at 95 per cent
, covering the questions and topics it projects for each BECE year.
The organisation also says its mock questions are kept secure ahead of its examinations, with measures in place to prevent questions from its examination bank from leaking online before the mock examination.
Schools can choose hard-copy or soft-copy mock papers
Education-News Consult says schools have flexibility in how they receive and administer the mock examinations.
The organisation provides printed mock papers on what it describes as high-quality paper, together with the resources required for the examination.
Schools that prefer digital materials can choose soft copies instead.
Schools can also decide whether they want Education-News Consult to handle marking and generate reports or whether they want to conduct the mock examinations and manage the marking themselves.
The School Mock Service page for more information is
https://skulmanager.com/schoolmock/request_mock.php
BECE preparation remains the focus
Education-News Consult says its services are aimed at schools and parents preparing students in Grade 9 at the Junior High School level for the BECE.
For schools, the mock examinations provide an opportunity to test students under examination conditions and assess their level of preparation.
For parents, the Home Mock service provides a separate option for assessing students from home while still allowing examination scripts to be submitted for marking and results to be accessed online.
The organisation says its services are intended to support the preparation of students whose schools and parents are working towards stronger performance at the BECE.
Education-News Consult directs schools and parents to Skulmanager.com
Education-News Consult has directed schools and parents interested in its mock examination services to
Skulmanager.com
, where its School Mock and Home Mock services are available.
The organisation says parents and schools can choose the service that suits their needs, whether they require school-based mock examinations, home assessment, printed examination papers, soft copies, marking or performance reports.
Education-News Consult can also be contacted by
call or WhatsApp on 0550360658
for information about its BECE Mock services.
With both school and home options available, the organisation is presenting its BECE Mock services as a preparation platform for schools and parents looking to assess students before the actual examination.
