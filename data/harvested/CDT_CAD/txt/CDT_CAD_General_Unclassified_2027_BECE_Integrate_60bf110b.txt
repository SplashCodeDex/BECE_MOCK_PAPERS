Candidates preparing for the 2027 Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) have been provided with comprehensive examination-style questions and assessment layouts following the release of the August 2026 home mock test package by Education-News Consult.
The latest Integrated Science test paper is structured to mirror the standard West African Examinations Council (WAEC) format, spanning a duration of two hours and ten minutes
. It requires students to answer Question 1 in Section A, which carries 40 marks, and select any other three questions from Section B, which is worth 60 marks, bringing the total obtainable score to 100 marks
.
Core Scientific Topics and Practical Competencies Tested
The assessment places significant emphasis on core scientific concepts drawn from the basic education curriculum, testing students across biology, chemistry, physics, and agricultural science.
In Section A, candidates are evaluated on morphological identification and vector control methods associated with common household pests such as the housefly
. The test further explores cellular biology by requiring students to differentiate between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, identify specific cell organelles including lysosomes and mitochondria, and state their structural similarities and differences
.
Physics and applied electricity form another major component of the assessment. Questions challenge learners to identify methods of magnetization, such as electrical wiring setups for nails, and list standard properties of materials used in electrical conducting wires
. Basic science practical skills are also evaluated through soil science experiments, where pupils are expected to interpret water retention levels across different soil sample set-ups, state observations, and draw valid scientific conclusions
.
Advanced Problem-Solving in Section B
Section B stretches candidates through analytical and calculation-based questions across diverse disciplines
. Chemistry items require students to determine sub-atomic particles—protons, neutrons, and electrons—from given atomic and mass numbers, and illustrate electronic configurations using labeled shell diagrams
.
Agricultural science questions focus on practical farm management techniques, exploring why cover crops like Centrosema are cultivated within oil palm plantations and why peanut crop rotation is deployed on farmlands infected with witchweed
. Energy transformation processes during hydroelectric power generation at a dam and calculations involving potential energy, kinetic energy, and force are also featured to test quantitative problem-solving skills
.
Education stakeholders note that regular practice with these examiner-set questions helps candidates bridge knowledge gaps, master time management, and build confidence ahead of the national examination. Teachers and parents are encouraged to use the accompanying mock questions and marking schemes to evaluate learner progress and target weak academic areas.
