BECE Home Mock Information Session to be Held Online on October 4
by
Wisdom K.E Hammond
·
October 4, 2026
Education-News Consult will hold its first online information session for parents and BECE candidates on Sunday, October 4, 2026, to explain its BECE Home Mock service, answer questions about the programme and introduce a special registration promotion running from October 4 to 10.
The online session, scheduled for
6:00 p.m. to 7:10 p.m.
, will give parents and students an opportunity to understand how the Home Mock works before deciding whether to enrol.
The session will be hosted on
Google Meet
, with members of the Education-News Consult team and examiners for the subjects covered by the mock service expected to participate.
Google Meet link: [INSERT GOOGLE MEET LINK HERE]
The information session is being organised as a direct opportunity for parents and students to ask questions about the Home Mock instead of relying on incomplete information circulating about mock examinations.
What parents and students will learn about the BECE Home Mock
Education-News Consult says the information session will provide a basic but detailed explanation of the Home Mock service available through
Skulmanager.com
.
Parents will be taken through how the service works, what candidates are expected to do, the subjects covered, the available pricing arrangements and how students receive assessment and feedback.
The session will also explain the special promotion being offered to parents and students who register for the mock service between
October 4 and October 10, 2026
.
The exact promotional price should be confirmed by Education-News Consult during the session and through the official registration information provided to parents.
SkulManager currently describes its Home Mock Service as a professional home-based assessment service for BECE candidates, with expert marking and performance tracking. (
skulmanager.com
)
Eight BECE subjects are covered
The Education-News Consult Home Mock programme covers the core and other BECE subjects that candidates are expected to prepare for.
The subjects covered are
English Language, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Computing, Religious and Moral Education, Creative Arts and Design, and Career Technology
.
The October 4 information session will give students a clearer understanding of what preparation in each subject should involve.
This is particularly important because performing well in a mock examination is not simply about attempting questions. Students need to understand the demands of the different papers, how to approach questions and where they are losing marks.
The subject examiners joining the online session will therefore have an important role in helping candidates understand what is expected in their respective subjects.
Examiners will speak directly to students and parents
One of the main features of the October 4 session is the participation of examiners for the subjects covered by the Home Mock.
Students will have the opportunity to hear directly from the people involved in setting and assessing the examinations about the expectations for the different subjects.
Parents will also benefit from the session because they will have an opportunity to understand what the Home Mock is designed to achieve and how they can support their wards during the preparation process.
For parents who may be unfamiliar with home-based mock examinations, the session provides an opportunity to ask practical questions before committing to the service.
Students, meanwhile, can use the session to gain a better understanding of what they should be doing now to prepare for the BECE.
Why the Home Mock is different from simply downloading BECE questions
A major reason parents should pay attention to the information session is the difference between having access to examination questions and participating in an organised assessment process.
Education-News Consult’s Home Mock service is designed as an assessment programme rather than simply a collection of questions.
SkulManager describes the Home Mock as a personalised, home-based assessment service that includes expert external marking and performance analysis.
That means the focus is not only on giving candidates questions to answer. The assessment is intended to provide information about how a candidate performs and where further preparation may be required.
For parents, this can make the mock more useful as a preparation tool because the candidate’s performance becomes part of the learning process.
The October 4–10 promotion gives parents a reason to act early
Education-News Consult will also use the information session to explain its
special promotion for registrations made from October 4 to October 10, 2026
.
Parents who have been considering enrolling their wards will therefore have an opportunity to understand the service first and then take advantage of the promotional registration period.
The promotion is particularly relevant to parents who want their wards to begin structured BECE preparation early rather than waiting until the examination is close.
Education-News Consult says the details of the promotion, including the applicable prices, will be explained during the information session.
Parents should therefore join the session before registering so they can understand the available options and the applicable promotional arrangements.
How much does the BECE Home Mock cost?
The October 4 information session will provide parents with the current prices for the Home Mock service and explain what each registration option covers.
Because the promotion runs specifically from
October 4 to October 10, 2026
, parents should rely on the prices communicated by Education-News Consult during the session or through its official registration channel rather than on old prices that may appear in previous posts.
This is also important because parents should understand exactly what they are paying for before enrolling their wards.
How parents can prepare for the information session
Parents do not need to wait until after the session to start thinking about their wards’ preparation.
They can join with questions about the subjects, how the Home Mock is administered, how marking works, how students receive feedback, the cost of registration and how to enrol through Skulmanager.com.
Students should also attend because the session is not intended for parents alone.
The participation of subject examiners means candidates can hear directly about what is expected of them and use the information to approach their preparation more seriously.
