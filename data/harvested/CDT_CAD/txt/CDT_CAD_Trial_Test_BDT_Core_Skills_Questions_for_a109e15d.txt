New Trial Test BDT Core Skills Questions for 2022 BECE Candidates for candidates preparing for the Junior High School leaaving examination is here.
The Basic Design and Technology (BDT) paper is one of the few subjects at the JHS level which has options, however, most students do not do well. In this article, we share questions we have tagged Trial Test BDT Core Skills Questions for 2022 BECE Candidates must be taken serious.
These are questions that often candidates find difficult to answer. We hope that they will be taken seriously as candidates prepare for the upcoming examination.
Trial Test BDT Core Skills Questions for 2022 BECE Candidates
If you cannot answer these questions, you need to sit up immediately,
Identify four kinds of food nutrients and name four food sources for each.
Explain the term “food” and describe four functions of food
State one function of each food nutrient.
For each of the following, identify two Deficiency signs and symptoms in children:
a. Lack of protein
b. Lack of carbohydrates
c. Lack of Vitamin C
d. Lack of Vitamin D and calcium
e. Lack of iodine:
f. Lack of iron:
g. Lack of calcium:
5. Explain the terms Food Spoilage and Food Preservation.
6. Identify and explain four causes of food Spoilage
7. Explain four suitable methods for preserving food. Provide specific examples for each.
8. Give five reasons for cooking food
9. Identify and describe the basic moist methods of cooking
10. State four types of foods and three methods for their preparation
Candidates are advised to avoid writing lazy short answers. Lazy short answers do not help you earn the full marks for each questions you answer. If you want to make a good grade from the BED, write very good answers. Most BECE candidates fail to do well when the attempt Core Skills questions.
While preparing for the BECE, do well to get the basic BED knowledge right so that you can perform well.
