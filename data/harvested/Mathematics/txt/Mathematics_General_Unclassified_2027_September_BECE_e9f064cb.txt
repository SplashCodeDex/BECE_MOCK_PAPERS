Examiners Upload September BECE Home Mock Results in Four Core Subjects as 2027 Registration Opens
Examiners evaluating the September edition of the national BECE Home Mock have officially commenced uploading marked candidate scripts and results for Mathematics, Computing, Religious and Moral Education (RME), and English Language.
The evaluation process, managed by Education-News Consult through Ghana’s dedicated assessment platform, skulmanager.com, provides registered candidates and parents with immediate digital access to corrected answer scripts, examiner feedback and marked scripts for download, and performance breakdowns. On the same dashboard, parents can download the questions, marking schemes, and reports.
Parents with candidates enrolled in the September assessment cycle are advised to log in directly to their dedicated candidate portals on skulmanager.com to review their wards’ performance, inspect marked scripts, and track subject-specific progress.
The release of results for these four foundational subjects comes as Education-News Consult opens registration for candidates preparing for the May 2027 Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).
Operating since 2022, the BECE Home Mock remains Ghana’s only official home-based standardized assessment ecosystem. Designed to replicate the West African Examinations Council (WAEC) testing standard, the service allows candidates to sit for rigorous, unseen exam papers from home under structured time limits and parental supervision. Completed physical scripts are then scanned into single PDF documents per subject and uploaded to the portal for marking by professional external examiners.
Five Reasons Parents Enroll Candidates in the BECE Home Mock
Educational assessment data and feedback from participating households highlight five core reasons why parents consistently enroll their BECE-bound candidates in the program:
Diagnostic Performance Tracking:
Rather than waiting for the final examination, the monthly mock cycle identifies critical learning gaps across the Grade 7, 8, and 9 curriculum early, allowing targeted remediation before the main WAEC exam.
WAEC-Standard Examiner Marking:
Scripts are marked and annotated by professional external examiners who provide detailed corrections, subject-specific scores, and official marking schemes to guide revision.
Reduction of Exam Anxiety:
Sitting for regular, timed home examinations helps candidates build time-management discipline, understand question structures, and overcome examination fears well in advance.
The SkulManager Smart Dashboard Advantage:
The digital portal streamlines the entire process, allowing parents to instantly download standardized exam papers, submit scanned answer sheets, and view marked scripts without leaving home.
Progressive Curriculum Coverage:
The 2027 mock timetable layers subject topics systematically—starting with Basic 7 content and expanding progressively into Basic 8 and Basic 9—ensuring thorough revision across all subjects.
