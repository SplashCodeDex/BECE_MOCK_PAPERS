WASSCE 2026 · Paper 1
Henry must tell Cynthia that he believes in ______.
A
her
B
herself
C
him
D
themselves
Marking scheme
The pronoun must refer to Cynthia, who is female, and it is the object of the preposition 'in.' The correct object pronoun is therefore 'her,' not the reflexive 'herself.'
