Students preparing for the 2027 Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE), parents supporting revision at home, and schools looking for extra practice materials can now download a free 2026 August BECE Mathematics Mock Questions with the marking schemes from GhanaEducation.org.
The five-page mock paper, produced as an August 2026 practice examination, gives candidates the opportunity to experience both the objective and essay sections of a BECE-style Mathematics paper before sitting the real examination.
Rather than relying only on last-minute revision, the mock is designed to expose students to the kind of multi-topic questions that require careful thinking, problem-solving and time management.
What is inside the free 2027 BECE Mathematics Mock Paper?
The practice paper follows a familiar BECE-style structure with 40 objective questions and a 60-mark essay section, allowing students to practise answering different types of Mathematics questions under examination conditions.
The objective section covers several important Mathematics topics, including percentages, fractions, prime factors, lowest common multiples, highest common factors, time calculations, scale drawing, algebra, indices and statistics.
The essay section challenges students with longer working questions that test mathematical reasoning, including ratio and proportion, vectors, straight-line equations, sets, graph work, trigonometry and real-life financial calculations.
Sample questions students can expect
Some of the questions included in the free mock paper reflect the practical style of problems students are expected to solve during BECE preparation.
One essay question asks candidates to calculate the total profit shared between two business partners after dividing earnings in a 3:5 ratio, using the information that one partner received GH¢3,000 more than the other.
Another question requires students to find the distance of a boat from the foot of a 105-metre cliff using an angle of depression and trigonometry.
Students are also challenged to solve graph-based questions, including plotting the relation y = 5 − 2x, drawing a straight line and using the completed graph to determine unknown values.
In the objective section, candidates practise questions involving percentages, time calculations, common factors, fractions, word problems and number operations similar to those regularly encountered during BECE revision.
Why practising with mock examinations matters
Mock examinations help students move beyond memorising formulas by exposing them to mixed-topic questions that demand careful reading, calculation and decision-making.
Attempting a complete paper under timed conditions can help students identify weak topics earlier, improve speed and accuracy, build confidence before the final examination and become more familiar with the structure of BECE-style questions.
Parents can also use the paper to monitor revision at home, while teachers can incorporate selected questions into classroom discussions and extra classes.
How the BECE Home Mock helps parents prepare candidates
The free Mathematics mock forms part of the wider support offered through Education-News Consult’s BECE Home Mock Service, which is designed for parents who want structured preparation outside the classroom.
The Home Mock programme allows candidates to write scheduled mock examinations throughout the preparation period instead of waiting until the final weeks before BECE.
Students receive carefully prepared examination papers covering core BECE subjects, helping families track progress consistently as the examination approaches.
