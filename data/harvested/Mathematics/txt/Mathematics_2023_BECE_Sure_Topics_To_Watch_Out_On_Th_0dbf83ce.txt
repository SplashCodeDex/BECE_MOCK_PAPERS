2023 BECE Sure Topics To Watch Out On Thursday – Mathematics And ICT
by
Rexford Dede Baah-Nyarkoh
·
August 8, 2023
On Thursday, 10th August, 2023, Mathematics and Information And Communication Technology(ICT) papers will be written. Mathematics will be written first followed by Information And Communication Technology(ICT).
Here are some likely topics for the examination for both papers
.
Mathematics
1. Venn Diagram
2. Statistics (Frequency distribution table, stem and leaf, pie chart, bar chart)
3. Construction
4. Transformation
5. Linear graph
6. Percentages( profit, loss, discount)
7. Ratio and proportion
8. Area and perimeter, volume
9. Angles
10. Fraction
11.. Integers
12. Linear equation
13. Decimal fraction
Information And Communication Technology
1. Spreadsheet applications(Microsoft excel, access point etc).The cell, merge,formula, column and others
2. Web browsers and service providers (Microsoft word, opera, Google chrome and others)
3. Classifications of computer( by side, data handling, functionality etc).minicomputer, supper computer, workstation computer,micro computer etc.
4. Uses, importance,hazard and threat of a computer.
5. Part and components of a computer.( The hardware eg the keyboard, mouse,monitor system unit etc.) Software components.
6. The Internet (internet etiquette, the use of computer laboratory, fraudulent).
7.computing devices.( computer, radio, PAS Mobile phone)
8 Storage devices and their uses (peripherals, input and output devices
Mathematics 2 (Essay) will start at exactly 9.00 am and end at 10:00 a.m. Mathematics 1 (Objective) will also start at 10:00 a.m. and end on 11:00am.
Candidates will now take a break from 11:00 am to 1:00pm.
