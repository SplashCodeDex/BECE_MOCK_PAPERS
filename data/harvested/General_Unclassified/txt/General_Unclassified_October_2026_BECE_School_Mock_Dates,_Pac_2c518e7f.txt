October 2026 BECE School Mock: Schools Can Choose Special Dates or Join October 19–23 General Mock
by
Wisdom K.E Hammond
·
September 29, 2026
Schools preparing Junior High School (JHS) students for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) can register for the October 2026 BECE School Mock organised by Education-News Consult, with schools given the option of choosing their own examination date or participating in the provider’s general mock schedule.
The October service has been structured around two date options: the
Special Date School Mock
, where individual schools determine when they want to conduct their mock examination, and the
General Date School Mock
, scheduled for
October 19 to 23, 2026
.
Education-News Consult says the service is designed for schools, churches and non-governmental organisations (NGOs) seeking assessment materials and examination support for JHS students.
How the October 2026 BECE School Mock works
Under the Special Date option, a school determines the date on which it wants to conduct its mock examination and places an order for its preferred mock package.
This means schools do not have to follow the general October timetable if they need a different examination date. The school selects its preferred date and orders the appropriate service package.
For schools choosing the General Date option, Education-News Consult determines the examination date. Schools then place their orders and conduct the mock using the approved general mock date.
The general October 2026 BECE School Mock is scheduled to run from
October 19 to 23, 2026
.
The two options give schools a choice between arranging the assessment around their own school schedule and joining the centrally determined October mock period.
Three October BECE School Mock packages available
Education-News Consult is offering three main service options for the school mock.
The
Hard Copy Standard Mock
costs
GHS35 per candidate
. The package includes printed examination questions, official scannable answer sheets and marking schemes.
The
Hard Copy Premium + Marking service
costs
GHS55 per candidate
. In addition to the examination materials, the service includes professional marking, a Smart Result Analysis Dashboard and Chief Examiner’s Performance Reports, according to the service information provided.
The third option is the
Soft Copy Bulk Service
, which costs
GHS250 per mock
. It provides schools with digital examination questions and comprehensive marking schemes, with unlimited school printing rights. The service is tailored for JHS 1, JHS 2 and JHS 3.

What schools need to provide when registering
Schools or organisations seeking to register are required to provide basic institutional and contact information through the mock quotation process.
The information requested includes the
school or organisation name, town, digital address or location, authorised contact person’s name, phone number and student count
.
Schools also select their preferred package before the estimated cost is generated.
The phone number provided is used for SMS invoice purposes.
Schools that have already placed an order can also use the
invoice retrieval
option provided by the service.
Delivery arrangements for hard copy mocks
The service information states that the displayed total does not include shipping costs.
For schools choosing the Premium service, Education-News Consult indicates that the package requires three delivery trips: delivery of the examination materials, collection of completed materials and return of the processed materials.
Schools are therefore advised to contact Education-News Consult for applicable delivery rates before finalising their arrangements.

Assessment and reporting options
The mock service combines examination materials with different levels of marking and performance reporting depending on the package selected.
The standard package provides schools with the examination papers, scannable answer sheets and marking schemes, while the Premium package adds professional marking and performance analysis.
The Premium service also includes access to a Smart Result Analysis Dashboard and Chief Examiner’s Performance Reports, which are intended to help schools examine student performance after the mock.
Education-News Consult describes the broader service as an institutional assessment solution for schools, churches and NGOs and states that it has examined more than 50,000 students and worked with more than 200 partner schools. These figures are claims made in the service information and have not been independently verified in this report.
How to register for the October 2026 BECE School Mock
A school interested in the October mock can begin the registration process by providing its institutional details, selecting either the Special Date or General Date arrangement and choosing one of the three available service packages.
Schools choosing the Special Date option should determine the date they want to conduct the mock before placing their order.
Those opting for the General Date service should plan around the
October 19–23, 2026
examination period set by Education-News Consult.
For registration support and enquiries, the service provides
0550360658
as its contact number.
With the October mock period approaching, schools that want to use the General Date option will need to plan their examination arrangements around the approved October 19–23 schedule, while schools requiring a different date can use the Special Date option and organise the mock around their own timetable.
