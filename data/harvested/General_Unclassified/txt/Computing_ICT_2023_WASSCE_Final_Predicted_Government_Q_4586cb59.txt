2023 WASSCE: Final Predicted Government Questions To Help You Get Grade ‘A’
by
Rexford Dede Baah-Nyarkoh
·
May 24, 2023
The 2023 WASSCE Government paper will be written on Monday, 4th September, 2023. Candidates should solve the following likely questions to help them secure the best grades.
Explain nationalism.
Differentiate between proto – Nationalism and militant-nationalism
Explain any five strategies adopted by the Nationalists to achieve self-rule in the Gold Coast after the Second World War (1945)
What are the main demands, and achievements of WASU, NCBWA, and ARPS
ldentify any 3 newspapers funded by the nationalist in Gold Coast.
Explain any four roles played by local newspapers in the struggle against colonial rule
Explain any five internal factors that accounted for nationalism after the Second World War.
What is Militant/Modern Nationalism?
Explain any four external factors that promoted Nationalist Activities on Gold Coast After the second Word war.
Identify any four aims and objectives of the Ecowas
State any five institutions of the Ecowas
State any three benefits Ghana derives from being a member of the Ecowas
Explain any four contributions of Ghana towards Ecowas
Outline any five achievenments of Ecowas
State any three problems facing the Ecowas
What factors determine the foreign policy of your country
Define the French policy of Assimilation
Explain any four features of the French Policy of Assimilation
Define the French policy of Assimilation.
Identify any four qualifications for granting French citizenship during the colonial period.
Explain any five reasons why the French policy of Assimilation failed
Outline five privileges that French West African citizens enjoyed under the French colonial administration.
What were the functions of the prime minister under the 1969 constitutions of Ghana
What were the causes of 1948 riots in Ghana?
Assess the achievements of the armed forces revolutionary council of Ghana.
Why did Kwame Nkrumah break with the United Gold Coast Convention?
Discuss the reasons for the introduction of one party system in Ghana under President Kwame Nkrumah
Five roles of the chiefs during the pre-colonial traditional system
Five ways were the pre-colonial traditional system democratic
Six duties of the chiefs during the colonial traditional system
Five ways were the Traditional African political system undemocratic
Highlight five roles of the modern or contemporary chiefs in Ghana
Five causes of chieftaincy isputes in Ghana
Outline five reasons why chiefs must abstain from partisan politics
Explain colonialism
State five factors that accounted for colonialism in West Africa
What is an indirect rule?
Explain any four positive effects of colonialism
What is crown colony system
Explain any five reasons that accounted for British adoption of indirect rule in West Africa.
