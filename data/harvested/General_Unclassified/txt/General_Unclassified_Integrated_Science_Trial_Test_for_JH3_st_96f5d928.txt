Teachers, schools, and JHS 3 students can now attempt to solve these Integrated Science Trial Tests or use them for revising lessons or topics from which the questions were set.
Note that these questions are mainly from JHS1 and we expect all JHS3 students to be able to solve these questions. Students are advised to revise these topics and find the answers for them if they are challenged in solving them.
Integrated Science Trial Test for JH3 students (BECE Trial Test 1)
Choose The Correct Answer From The Alternatives A – D
1. Which of the following is an example of converting chemical energy to heat energy.
a) the use of flash light
b) bringing firewood
c) ringing school bell
d) d. drying clothes
2. Which of the following convert solar energy to chemical energy.
a) Plants
b) batteries
c) bells
d) Flash light.

a) wedge

d) inclined plane

BECE Integrated Science Mock 1 (Free Online Mock)
Integrated Science Trial Test for JH3
SECTION B
Answer The Following Questions Correctly
QUESTION ONE – COMPULSORY
a) Draw and label the specimen (bony fish) and explain how it breathe in its habitat.
b) Write note about the following personality and his achievement;
I. Isaac newton
II. Albert Einstein
c) What is mal nutrition?
d) List four diseases that are communicable.
Choose any four questions from the options below.
All questions are of same mark.
QUESTION TWO
a) Define the machines with two examples
b) Give four differences between climate and weather
c) What is efficiency of machine?
QUESTION THREE.
a) State the law of reflections
b) Numerate four ways to generate electricity
c) Draw and label capacitor or dry cell.
QUESTION FOUR
a) Give four ways to care for machines
b) The work input to a machine is 80J whereas the work output is 50J. Find the efficiency of the machines.
c) Draw the electrical circuit with three cells in a series connection.
QUESTIONS FIVE
a) List two differences between pastoral farming and crop rotations
b) A body of mass 1.5kg hangs at a height of 35m. Calculates the potential energy.
c) State newton’s third and second law of motion
QUESTION SIX.
a) List and explain four types of force.
b) Give four benefits of mirror
c) With an aid of a diagram, explain the types of reflections.
