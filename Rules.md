# Staralex BECE Mock Papers — Engineering & Content Rules

## 1. General Principles
- Plain, simple English for easy understanding. Unpack every technical concept.
- International British spelling/notation (*factorise*, *brackets*, *BODMAS*, *trapezium*, *GH₵*).
- Authentic WAEC/BECE past-paper questions take priority over invented questions.

## 2. Diagram Asset Standard & Strict Format Policy
- **Authoring:** High-fidelity past-paper diagrams are authored exclusively in LaTeX / TikZ under `data/diagrams/math_redrawn/`.
- **Allowed Formats:** Strictly **`.tex`** (source code) and **`.png`** (300 DPI raster render).
- **Prohibited Formats:** Strictly **NO `.pdf`** and **NO `.svg`** files for diagrams.
  - High-DPI concerns do not justify keeping SVGs or diagram PDFs.
  - Any intermediate `.pdf` or `.svg` files generated by compilers (`pdflatex`, `dvisvgm`, etc.) must be deleted immediately after rasterization to PNG.
  - No diagram `.pdf` or `.svg` may ever be committed, stored, or retained in diagram asset directories.
- **Mandatory Visual Inspection:** Before embedding any diagram into mock papers or lesson exercises, inspect the 300 DPI `.png` to verify zero label collisions, correct line weights, and visual fidelity.
